# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our
project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Professional and Ethical Engineering Standard

`minecraft-recon-framework` is developed strictly for network protocol research,
systems administration, and authorized security auditing. Contributions or
issues promoting unauthorized intrusion, denial-of-service, or abuse of
third-party infrastructure shall be closed and removed immediately.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or unethical behavior may be reported to the
repository maintainers via GitHub Issues.
